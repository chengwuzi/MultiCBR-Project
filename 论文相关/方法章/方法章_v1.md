## English Manuscript

## 4 Methodology

### 4.1 Task Definition and Framework Overview

Let $\mathcal U$, $\mathcal B$, and $\mathcal I$ denote the sets of users, predefined bundles, and items, respectively. The available training data comprise three binary matrices: user-bundle interactions $\mathbf X\in\{0,1\}^{|\mathcal U|\times|\mathcal B|}$, user-item interactions $\mathbf Y\in\{0,1\}^{|\mathcal U|\times|\mathcal I|}$, and bundle-item affiliations $\mathbf Z\in\{0,1\}^{|\mathcal B|\times|\mathcal I|}$. An entry equal to one indicates an observed interaction or an item belonging to a bundle. Given these relations, our task is to estimate a preference score $\hat y_{ub}$ for each unobserved user-bundle pair and rank candidate bundles for each user.

Figure 1 outlines DiGO. To address noise in U-B interactions, we first pretrain user and bundle representations, learn a user-conditioned denoiser over historical bundle representations, and use the refined preferences to reconstruct the U-B graph. The denoising objective incorporates a bundle-set consistency loss (SetLoss) to retain bundle-preference information during recovery. To address sparse U-B feedback, we then use the reconstructed U-B view as a common anchor for cross-view contrastive learning before representation fusion. The fused user and bundle representations produce the final recommendation scores.

We distinguish the observed matrix $\mathbf X$ from the reconstructed matrix $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$ throughout this section. The former defines users' training histories and ranking examples; the latter determines the U-B connections used for recommendation graph propagation. Pretraining, preference construction, and graph reconstruction use only training interactions.

### 4.2 Diffusion-Driven U-B Graph Denoising and Reconstruction

The purpose of this module is to evaluate an observed interaction in the context of the user's historical bundle preferences. We construct this context in a pretrained representation space, refine it through conditional denoising, and translate the resulting preference vector into a selection of graph connections.

#### 4.2.1 Pretraining and Historical Preference Construction

We pretrain LightGCN [12] on the observed U-B graph using Bayesian Personalized Ranking (BPR) [21]. The resulting user and bundle representations, denoted by $\bar{\mathbf e}_u,\bar{\mathbf e}_b\in\mathbb R^d$, remain fixed during graph reconstruction. Here, $d$ is the embedding dimension. These representations provide a common space for constructing historical preferences, conditioning the denoiser, and scoring interactions.

For user $u$, let $\mathcal N_u=\{b\in\mathcal B:x_{ub}=1\}$ be the set of historically interacted bundles. We summarize their representations by mean pooling:

$$
\mathbf p_{u,0}
=\frac{1}{|\mathcal N_u|}
\sum_{b\in\mathcal N_u}\bar{\mathbf e}_b.
\tag{1}
$$

The vector $\mathbf p_{u,0}$ represents the user's observed bundle-preference context. Mean pooling accommodates histories of different lengths while keeping the denoising input $d$-dimensional. We construct this vector for users in $\mathcal U_+=\{u\in\mathcal U:|\mathcal N_u|>0\}$. The subscript $0$ denotes the state before artificial noise is added; it does not assume that the observed history is free of interaction noise.

#### 4.2.2 User-Conditioned Diffusion Denoising

We train the denoiser to recover historical preference representations under different levels of perturbation. Following the Gaussian diffusion formulation [9], [10], let $T$ be the number of diffusion steps and $\beta_t\in(0,1)$ the noise variance at step $t$. We use a linear variance schedule and define $\alpha_t=1-\beta_t$ and $\bar\alpha_t=\prod_{s=1}^{t}\alpha_s$. A perturbed preference vector can be sampled directly as

$$
\mathbf p_{u,t}
=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,\mathbf p_{u,0}
+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\boldsymbol\epsilon,
\qquad
\boldsymbol\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(\mathbf 0,\mathbf I_d).
\tag{2}
$$

Here, $\mathbf I_d$ is the $d$-dimensional identity matrix. The cumulative coefficient $\bar\alpha_t$ controls how much historical information remains at each noise level.

To make recovery personalized, we condition the denoiser on the pretrained user representation $\bar{\mathbf e}_u$. We implement the denoiser $f_\theta$ as a multilayer perceptron (MLP), with parameters $\theta$, that predicts the unperturbed preference vector:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\mathbf p}_{u,0}
&=f_\theta(\mathbf p_{u,t},\bar{\mathbf e}_u,t),\\
f_\theta(\mathbf p,\mathbf c,t)
&=\operatorname{MLP}_\theta
\bigl([\mathbf p;\mathbf c;\mathbf p\odot\mathbf c;
|\mathbf p-\mathbf c|;\boldsymbol\psi(t)]\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
$$

In Eq. (3), $[\cdot;\cdot]$ denotes concatenation, $\odot$ is the element-wise product, and the absolute difference is also element-wise. The vector $\boldsymbol\psi(t)\in\mathbb R^{d_t}$ is a sinusoidal timestep embedding. The product and difference expose coordinate-wise agreement and discrepancy between the current preference vector and the user condition. Together with the timestep embedding, they allow the MLP to adapt recovery to both the user and the perturbation level. The input dimension is $4d+d_t$, and the output dimension is $d$.

During training, we uniformly sample $u\in\mathcal U_+$ and $t\in\{1,\ldots,T\}$ and draw Gaussian noise as in Eq. (2). The reconstruction objective is

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rec}}
=\mathbb E_{u,t,\boldsymbol\epsilon}
\left[
\frac{1}{d}
\left\|\widehat{\mathbf p}_{u,0}-\mathbf p_{u,0}\right\|_2^2
\right].
\tag{4}
$$

This objective learns a shared recovery function from perturbed historical preferences. The original representation supplies the reconstruction target, while the pretrained user embedding provides personalized context.

#### 4.2.3 Bundle-Set Consistency in Denoising

Reconstructing the pooled vector provides a basic denoising objective, but subsequent interaction selection depends on the recovered vector's compatibility with individual historical bundles. We therefore introduce SetLoss to connect preference recovery with the bundle representations that will be used for graph reconstruction.

Let $\operatorname{sim}(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=\mathbf a^\top\mathbf b/(\|\mathbf a\|_2\|\mathbf b\|_2)$ denote cosine similarity, with a small numerical safeguard for zero norms. SetLoss encourages average directional agreement between the recovered preference and the user's historical bundle representations:

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{set}}
=\mathbb E_{u,t,\boldsymbol\epsilon}
\left[
\frac{1}{|\mathcal N_u|}
\sum_{b\in\mathcal N_u}
\left(1-\operatorname{sim}
\left(\widehat{\mathbf p}_{u,0},\bar{\mathbf e}_b\right)\right)
\right].
\tag{5}
$$

The two objectives emphasize different aspects of recovery. Equation (4) matches the coordinates of the pooled representation, whereas Eq. (5) measures agreement with each historical bundle after vector normalization. This keeps the recovered preference connected to the historical bundle set in the same similarity geometry used for interaction scoring. Averaging over each user's history also prevents the loss from increasing merely because that user has more observed interactions.

We optimize the denoiser with

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm D}
=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rec}}
+\lambda_{\mathrm{set}}\mathcal L_{\mathrm{set}},
\tag{6}
$$

where $\lambda_{\mathrm{set}}\geq 0$ controls the consistency constraint. SetLoss serves as a task-specific regularizer within the denoising framework: it encourages preservation of bundle-preference context while the reconstruction term learns recovery from perturbations.

#### 4.2.4 Preference Refinement and Graph Reconstruction

After learning the denoiser, we use it to refine the historical preference vector for interaction scoring. This stage employs a deterministic refinement procedure initialized with $\mathbf p_{u,0}$. Specifically,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf r_{u,T}&=\mathbf p_{u,0},\\
\mathbf r_{u,t-1}
&=f_\theta(\mathbf r_{u,t},\bar{\mathbf e}_u,t),
\qquad t=T,T-1,\ldots,1,\\
\mathbf p_u^\star&=\mathbf r_{u,0}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
$$

The states $\mathbf r_{u,t}$ are refinement iterates, distinct from the forward-corrupted training states $\mathbf p_{u,t}$. Each iteration feeds the current estimate back into the denoiser with the next timestep condition. Thus, graph reconstruction applies the learned recovery network to an observed preference context, without sampling a Gaussian starting state.

We measure how closely each observed bundle matches the refined preference:

$$
s_{ub}
=\operatorname{sim}(\mathbf p_u^\star,\bar{\mathbf e}_b),
\qquad b\in\mathcal N_u.
\tag{8}
$$

For each user, we retain the highest-scoring $\kappa_u=\min(\kappa,|\mathcal N_u|)$ bundles, where the positive integer $\kappa$ controls the maximum retained degree:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde{\mathcal N}_u
&=\underset{b\in\mathcal N_u}{\operatorname{TopK}}
\left(s_{ub},\kappa_u\right),\\
\widetilde x_{ub}
&=\mathbf 1\!\left[b\in\widetilde{\mathcal N}_u\right].
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
$$

Here, $\operatorname{TopK}$ returns bundle indices and $\mathbf 1[\cdot]$ is the indicator function. Users with at most $\kappa$ observed bundles retain their full histories; users outside $\mathcal U_+$ have no retained U-B edges. The binary matrix $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$ defines the reconstructed U-B graph. Selection is restricted to observed interactions, so this step changes the graph connections used for propagation rather than assigning new interaction labels. Through Eqs. (1)–(9), pretrained collaborative information, personalized denoising, and bundle-set consistency jointly support preference-based graph reconstruction.

### 4.3 Pre-Fusion Cross-View Contrastive Learning

Sparse U-B histories provide limited evidence for learning personalized bundle preferences. We use item-level behavior and bundle composition to supply complementary learning signals, explicitly connecting their representations to the reconstructed U-B view before fusion.

#### 4.3.1 View-Specific Representations

We adopt the three-view recommendation backbone of MultiCBR [5], using $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$, $\mathbf Y$, and $\mathbf Z$ for the U-B, U-I, and B-I propagation graphs, respectively. The backbone shares trainable initial user, bundle, and item embeddings across views, while each view has its own propagation path. These trainable embeddings are separate from the fixed pretrained representations used in Section 4.2.

Each graph encoder applies $L$ layers of symmetrically normalized neighborhood propagation following LightGCN [12]. We combine the initial embeddings and the row-normalized propagated representations using view-specific layer coefficients. The U-B encoder directly yields $\mathbf e_u^{\mathrm{UB}}$ and $\mathbf e_b^{\mathrm{UB}}$. The U-I encoder yields user and item representations, while the B-I encoder yields bundle and item representations. The missing entity representation in each auxiliary view is obtained by mean pooling:

$$
\mathbf e_b^{\mathrm{UI}}
=\frac{1}{|\mathcal I_b|}
\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_b}\mathbf e_i^{\mathrm{UI}},
\qquad
\mathbf e_u^{\mathrm{BI}}
=\frac{1}{|\mathcal I_u|}
\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_u}\mathbf e_i^{\mathrm{BI}},
\tag{10}
$$

where $\mathcal I_b=\{i:z_{bi}=1\}$ contains the items in bundle $b$, and $\mathcal I_u=\{i:y_{ui}=1\}$ contains the items interacted with by user $u$. An empty neighborhood contributes a zero vector. Consequently, every user and bundle has three view-specific representations in $\mathbb R^d$. The U-I view reflects item-level collaborative behavior, whereas the B-I view emphasizes bundle composition.

During training, the backbone applies small representation perturbations, as in the noise augmentation setting of MultiCBR [5]. For a propagated or pooled vector $\mathbf h$, the perturbation has the form $\eta_v\,\operatorname{sign}(\mathbf h)\odot\boldsymbol\xi/\|\boldsymbol\xi\|_2$, where the entries of $\boldsymbol\xi$ are sampled uniformly from $(0,1)$ and $\eta_v$ controls its magnitude for the corresponding relation. We omit the perturbation notation from the view representations for readability. These augmentations are disabled at evaluation.

#### 4.3.2 Cross-View Objectives Anchored in U-B

We use the U-B representations learned from $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$ as the reference for connecting the auxiliary views. For a user, a positive pair consists of that user's U-B representation and its U-I or B-I counterpart; the same construction applies to bundles. Representations of other entities of the same type provide in-batch negatives.

To express the objective compactly, consider two representation matrices $\mathbf A$ and $\mathbf B$ with $m$ corresponding rows $\mathbf a_j$ and $\mathbf b_j$. We use the InfoNCE objective [22]:

$$
\ell_\tau(\mathbf A,\mathbf B)
=-\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}
\log
\frac{\exp(\operatorname{sim}(\mathbf a_j,\mathbf b_j)/\tau)}
{\sum_{k=1}^{m}
\exp(\operatorname{sim}(\mathbf a_j,\mathbf b_k)/\tau)}.
\tag{11}
$$

The temperature $\tau>0$ controls sensitivity to similarity differences. The corresponding row is the positive candidate; all other rows are negative candidates. The denominator includes the positive candidate.

Let $\mathcal S_U$ and $\mathcal S_B$ be the sets of unique users and unique positive bundles in a recommendation minibatch. For view $v\in\{\mathrm{UB},\mathrm{UI},\mathrm{BI}\}$, let $\mathbf E_U^v$ and $\mathbf E_B^v$ contain the representations of these users and bundles, respectively, with a consistent entity order across views. The pre-fusion objective is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm A}
={}&\ell_{\tau_a}(\mathbf E_U^{\mathrm{UB}},\mathbf E_U^{\mathrm{UI}})
+\ell_{\tau_a}(\mathbf E_U^{\mathrm{UB}},\mathbf E_U^{\mathrm{BI}})\\
&+\ell_{\tau_a}(\mathbf E_B^{\mathrm{UB}},\mathbf E_B^{\mathrm{UI}})
+\ell_{\tau_a}(\mathbf E_B^{\mathrm{UB}},\mathbf E_B^{\mathrm{BI}}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
$$

Here, $\tau_a$ is the cross-view temperature. Deduplication prevents repeated occurrences of the same entity from being treated as negatives in this objective. A term is skipped if its entity set contains fewer than two members.

Equation (12) encourages agreement for the same user or bundle across U-B–U-I and U-B–B-I pairs while preserving discrimination between entities. The U-B view is the common anchor in constructing the objective, and gradients update both representations in each pair. These constraints allow item-level behavioral and compositional evidence to participate directly in bundle-preference learning, providing additional training signals beyond an individual user's limited U-B history.

### 4.4 Prediction and Model Optimization

We combine the three representations of each entity using nonnegative view coefficients $\omega_v$, with $\sum_{v\in\mathcal V}\omega_v=1$ and $\mathcal V=\{\mathrm{UB},\mathrm{UI},\mathrm{BI}\}$. The final preference score is the inner product of the fused representations:

$$
\mathbf e_x
=\sum_{v\in\mathcal V}\omega_v\mathbf e_x^v,
\quad x\in\{u,b\},
\qquad
\hat y_{ub}=\mathbf e_u^\top\mathbf e_b.
\tag{13}
$$

The layer and view coefficients are hyperparameters. Representation fusion and the fused-space contrastive regularizer are components of the recommendation backbone [5]. For completeness, let $\mathbf H_U$ and $\mathbf H_B$ contain the fused user and positive-bundle representations in minibatch order. The backbone self-contrastive regularizer used here is

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm C}
=\frac{1}{2}
\left[
\ell_{\tau_c}(\mathbf H_U,\mathbf H_U)
+\ell_{\tau_c}(\mathbf H_B,\mathbf H_B)
\right],
\tag{14}
$$

where $\tau_c$ is its temperature. Each row is paired with itself in the numerator, and the denominator contains all rows of the corresponding minibatch matrix. This term regularizes separation in the fused representation space; $\mathcal L_{\mathrm A}$ explicitly connects the different relation views before fusion.

For a minibatch $\mathcal Q$ of ranking triplets $(u,b^+,b^-)$, the positive bundle satisfies $x_{ub^+}=1$ and the negative bundle is sampled from $\mathcal B\setminus\mathcal N_u$. We optimize BPR [21] together with the two regularization objectives:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{BPR}}
&=-\frac{1}{|\mathcal Q|}
\sum_{(u,b^+,b^-)\in\mathcal Q}
\log\sigma\!\left(\hat y_{ub^+}-\hat y_{ub^-}\right),\\
\mathcal L_{\mathrm R}
&=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{BPR}}
+\lambda_a\mathcal L_{\mathrm A}
+\lambda_c\mathcal L_{\mathrm C}
+\lambda_r\|\Theta\|_2^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
$$

Here, $\sigma$ is the sigmoid function, $\Theta$ comprises the trainable initial user, bundle, and item embeddings of the recommendation backbone, and $\lambda_a,\lambda_c,\lambda_r\geq0$ balance the loss terms. Ranking examples continue to come from $\mathbf X$, even when their edges are not selected for $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$. Graph reconstruction therefore filters propagation connections while preserving the observed feedback used for ranking.

Training alternates between graph reconstruction and recommendation learning. After offline pretraining, each epoch first updates the denoiser using $\mathcal L_{\mathrm D}$ over the active users. We then reconstruct $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$ with the updated denoiser and optimize the recommendation backbone using $\mathcal L_{\mathrm R}$ over ranking minibatches. The denoiser and backbone have separate optimizers; the reconstructed graph is held fixed during the recommendation updates within an epoch. Discrete edge selection does not propagate ranking gradients back into the denoiser.

For recommendation, we compute and cache the final fused embeddings using the reconstructed graph associated with the selected model. Candidate bundles are ranked by Eq. (13), with training-observed bundles excluded. The denoiser is used for graph reconstruction and is not evaluated separately for each candidate bundle.

### 4.5 Computational Complexity

Let $M=|\mathcal E_{\mathrm{UB}}|$ be the number of observed U-B edges, and let $E=|\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{\mathrm{UB}}|+|\mathcal E_{\mathrm{UI}}|+|\mathcal E_{\mathrm{BI}}|$ be the total number of edges used by the three propagation graphs. Let $Q$ be the number of recommendation minibatches per epoch, $m$ their maximum size, and $C_f$ the cost of a denoiser evaluation for one user. For a fixed-depth MLP whose hidden and timestep dimensions are proportional to $d$, $C_f=O(d^2)$.

With one denoiser-training pass and one graph refresh per epoch, the main time cost is

$$
O\!\left(
(T+1)|\mathcal U_+|C_f
+M\bigl(d+\log(1+\kappa)\bigr)
+Q\bigl(LEd+m^2d\bigr)
\right).
\tag{16}
$$

The first term accounts for denoiser training and iterative preference refinement. The second includes history pooling, SetLoss evaluation, observed-edge scoring, and bounded top-$\kappa$ selection. The remaining terms cover graph propagation and minibatch contrastive computations. Offline LightGCN pretraining adds a graph-propagation cost of $O(LMd)$ per propagation pass. Since $|\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{\mathrm{UB}}|\leq M$, graph reconstruction does not densify the propagation graph or require scoring all $|\mathcal U||\mathcal B|$ pairs.

The trainable parameters occupy $O((|\mathcal U|+|\mathcal B|+|\mathcal I|)d+P_\theta)$ space, where $P_\theta$ is the denoiser parameter count. Fixed pretrained user and bundle embeddings additionally require $O((|\mathcal U|+|\mathcal B|)d)$ storage. Training also stores sparse graphs, intermediate graph representations, and temporary minibatch buffers. Once fused embeddings are cached, scoring one user-bundle pair costs $O(d)$.

## 中文对照稿

## 4 方法

### 4.1 任务定义与框架概述

令 $\mathcal U$、$\mathcal B$ 和 $\mathcal I$ 分别表示用户、预定义捆绑和物品的集合。可用的训练数据包含三个二值矩阵：用户–捆绑交互矩阵 $\mathbf X\in\{0,1\}^{|\mathcal U|\times|\mathcal B|}$、用户–物品交互矩阵 $\mathbf Y\in\{0,1\}^{|\mathcal U|\times|\mathcal I|}$，以及捆绑–物品归属矩阵 $\mathbf Z\in\{0,1\}^{|\mathcal B|\times|\mathcal I|}$。矩阵元素为 1 表示存在相应的观测交互，或某物品属于某捆绑。给定这些关系，我们的任务是为每个未观测的用户–捆绑对估计偏好得分 $\hat y_{ub}$，并为每个用户对候选捆绑进行排序。

图 1 展示了 DiGO 的整体框架。针对 U-B 交互中的噪声，我们首先预训练用户和捆绑表示，在历史捆绑表示上学习用户条件去噪器，再利用提炼后的偏好重构 U-B 图。去噪目标中引入捆绑集合一致性损失（SetLoss），以在恢复过程中保留捆绑偏好信息。针对稀疏的 U-B 反馈，我们进一步以重构后的 U-B 视图为共同锚点，在表示融合之前开展跨视图对比学习。融合后的用户和捆绑表示用于计算最终推荐得分。

本章始终区分观测矩阵 $\mathbf X$ 与重构矩阵 $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$：前者定义用户的训练历史和排序样本，后者决定推荐阶段图传播所使用的 U-B 连接。预训练、偏好构建和图重构均仅使用训练交互。

### 4.2 扩散驱动的 U-B 图去噪与重构

本模块旨在结合用户的历史捆绑偏好，评估其已观测交互。为此，我们先在预训练表示空间中构建历史偏好，再通过条件去噪对其进行提炼，最后根据所得偏好向量筛选图连接。

#### 4.2.1 预训练与历史偏好构建

我们在观测 U-B 图上采用贝叶斯个性化排序（BPR）目标 [21] 预训练 LightGCN [12]。所得用户和捆绑表示分别记为 $\bar{\mathbf e}_u,\bar{\mathbf e}_b\in\mathbb R^d$，在图重构过程中保持固定，其中 $d$ 为嵌入维度。这些表示为历史偏好构建、去噪器的条件输入及交互评分提供统一的表示空间。

对于用户 $u$，令 $\mathcal N_u=\{b\in\mathcal B:x_{ub}=1\}$ 表示其历史交互捆绑集合。我们通过均值池化汇总这些捆绑的表示：

$$
\mathbf p_{u,0}
=\frac{1}{|\mathcal N_u|}
\sum_{b\in\mathcal N_u}\bar{\mathbf e}_b.
\tag{1}
$$

向量 $\mathbf p_{u,0}$ 表示用户已观测的捆绑偏好上下文。均值池化能够适应不同长度的交互历史，并使去噪输入保持为 $d$ 维。我们为 $\mathcal U_+=\{u\in\mathcal U:|\mathcal N_u|>0\}$ 中的用户构建该向量。下标 $0$ 表示加入人工噪声之前的状态，并不假定观测历史中不存在交互噪声。

#### 4.2.2 用户条件扩散去噪

我们训练去噪器，使其能够在不同扰动程度下恢复历史偏好表示。遵循高斯扩散建模方式 [9], [10]，令 $T$ 为扩散步数，$\beta_t\in(0,1)$ 为第 $t$ 步的噪声方差。我们采用线性方差调度，并定义 $\alpha_t=1-\beta_t$、$\bar\alpha_t=\prod_{s=1}^{t}\alpha_s$。扰动后的偏好向量可直接采样得到：

$$
\mathbf p_{u,t}
=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,\mathbf p_{u,0}
+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\boldsymbol\epsilon,
\qquad
\boldsymbol\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(\mathbf 0,\mathbf I_d).
\tag{2}
$$

其中，$\mathbf I_d$ 是 $d$ 维单位矩阵。累积系数 $\bar\alpha_t$ 控制各噪声水平下保留的历史信息比例。

为了实现个性化恢复，我们将预训练用户表示 $\bar{\mathbf e}_u$ 作为去噪器的条件输入。去噪器 $f_\theta$ 由参数为 $\theta$ 的多层感知机（MLP）实现，直接预测扰动前的偏好向量：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\mathbf p}_{u,0}
&=f_\theta(\mathbf p_{u,t},\bar{\mathbf e}_u,t),\\
f_\theta(\mathbf p,\mathbf c,t)
&=\operatorname{MLP}_\theta
\bigl([\mathbf p;\mathbf c;\mathbf p\odot\mathbf c;
|\mathbf p-\mathbf c|;\boldsymbol\psi(t)]\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
$$

式（3）中，$[\cdot;\cdot]$ 表示拼接，$\odot$ 表示逐元素乘积，绝对值差同样按元素计算。$\boldsymbol\psi(t)\in\mathbb R^{d_t}$ 为正弦时间步嵌入。乘积与差值显式提供当前偏好向量与用户条件在各维度上的一致性和差异信息；结合时间步嵌入，MLP 能够根据用户及扰动水平调整恢复过程。其输入维度为 $4d+d_t$，输出维度为 $d$。

训练时，我们均匀采样 $u\in\mathcal U_+$ 和 $t\in\{1,\ldots,T\}$，并按式（2）采样高斯噪声。重构目标为

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rec}}
=\mathbb E_{u,t,\boldsymbol\epsilon}
\left[
\frac{1}{d}
\left\|\widehat{\mathbf p}_{u,0}-\mathbf p_{u,0}\right\|_2^2
\right].
\tag{4}
$$

该目标从受扰动的历史偏好中学习共享的恢复函数。原始偏好表示提供重构目标，预训练用户嵌入则提供个性化上下文。

#### 4.2.3 去噪过程中的捆绑集合一致性

重构池化向量提供了基础去噪目标，但后续的交互筛选依赖于恢复向量与各个历史捆绑的匹配程度。因此，我们引入 SetLoss，将偏好恢复与图重构所使用的捆绑表示联系起来。

令 $\operatorname{sim}(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=\mathbf a^\top\mathbf b/(\|\mathbf a\|_2\|\mathbf b\|_2)$ 表示余弦相似度，并在实现中对零范数采用小量保护。SetLoss 鼓励恢复偏好与用户历史捆绑表示在平均意义上保持方向一致：

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{set}}
=\mathbb E_{u,t,\boldsymbol\epsilon}
\left[
\frac{1}{|\mathcal N_u|}
\sum_{b\in\mathcal N_u}
\left(1-\operatorname{sim}
\left(\widehat{\mathbf p}_{u,0},\bar{\mathbf e}_b\right)\right)
\right].
\tag{5}
$$

两个目标分别强调恢复过程的不同方面：式（4）匹配池化表示的坐标，式（5）则在向量归一化后度量恢复结果与各个历史捆绑的一致性。这样，恢复偏好与历史捆绑集合之间的联系便建立在交互评分所采用的相似度空间中。此外，对每个用户的历史取平均，可避免损失值仅因该用户观测交互较多而增大。

去噪器的优化目标为

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm D}
=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rec}}
+\lambda_{\mathrm{set}}\mathcal L_{\mathrm{set}},
\tag{6}
$$

其中，$\lambda_{\mathrm{set}}\geq 0$ 控制一致性约束的权重。SetLoss 是去噪框架中面向捆绑推荐任务的正则项：重构项学习从扰动中恢复偏好，一致性项则鼓励恢复过程保留捆绑偏好上下文。

#### 4.2.4 偏好提炼与图重构

学习去噪器后，我们利用它提炼历史偏好向量，以便进行交互评分。该阶段采用以 $\mathbf p_{u,0}$ 为初始状态的确定性迭代过程：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf r_{u,T}&=\mathbf p_{u,0},\\
\mathbf r_{u,t-1}
&=f_\theta(\mathbf r_{u,t},\bar{\mathbf e}_u,t),
\qquad t=T,T-1,\ldots,1,\\
\mathbf p_u^\star&=\mathbf r_{u,0}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
$$

$\mathbf r_{u,t}$ 是偏好提炼阶段的迭代状态，与训练阶段通过前向加噪得到的 $\mathbf p_{u,t}$ 不同。每次迭代将当前估计重新送入去噪器，并使用下一时间步条件。因此，图重构是在已观测偏好上下文上应用学得的恢复网络，而非从高斯初始状态开始生成。

我们计算各个已观测捆绑与提炼后偏好的匹配程度：

$$
s_{ub}
=\operatorname{sim}(\mathbf p_u^\star,\bar{\mathbf e}_b),
\qquad b\in\mathcal N_u.
\tag{8}
$$

对于每个用户，保留得分最高的 $\kappa_u=\min(\kappa,|\mathcal N_u|)$ 个捆绑，其中正整数 $\kappa$ 控制最大保留度数：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde{\mathcal N}_u
&=\underset{b\in\mathcal N_u}{\operatorname{TopK}}
\left(s_{ub},\kappa_u\right),\\
\widetilde x_{ub}
&=\mathbf 1\!\left[b\in\widetilde{\mathcal N}_u\right].
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
$$

这里，$\operatorname{TopK}$ 返回捆绑索引，$\mathbf 1[\cdot]$ 为指示函数。观测捆绑数不超过 $\kappa$ 的用户保留全部历史连接；不属于 $\mathcal U_+$ 的用户没有保留的 U-B 边。二值矩阵 $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$ 定义重构后的 U-B 图。筛选仅限于已观测交互，因此该步骤改变的是用于传播的图连接，而不为未观测的用户–捆绑对赋予新的交互标签。式（1）至式（9）将预训练协同信息、个性化去噪和捆绑集合一致性结合起来，实现基于偏好的图重构。

### 4.3 融合前跨视图对比学习

稀疏的 U-B 历史为个性化捆绑偏好学习提供的证据有限。我们利用物品级行为和捆绑组成补充学习信号，并在融合之前将其表示与重构后的 U-B 视图显式关联。

#### 4.3.1 各视图的表示

我们采用 MultiCBR [5] 的三视图推荐骨干，分别使用 $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$、$\mathbf Y$ 和 $\mathbf Z$ 构建 U-B、U-I 和 B-I 传播图。骨干在各视图间共享可训练的初始用户、捆绑和物品嵌入，各视图则具有各自的传播路径。这些可训练嵌入与第 4.2 节使用的固定预训练表示相互独立。

各图编码器采用 LightGCN [12] 式的对称归一化邻域传播，共进行 $L$ 层。我们通过各视图的层权重组合初始嵌入和按行归一化后的各层传播表示。U-B 编码器直接得到 $\mathbf e_u^{\mathrm{UB}}$ 和 $\mathbf e_b^{\mathrm{UB}}$；U-I 编码器得到用户和物品表示；B-I 编码器得到捆绑和物品表示。每个辅助视图中缺失的实体表示通过均值池化获得：

$$
\mathbf e_b^{\mathrm{UI}}
=\frac{1}{|\mathcal I_b|}
\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_b}\mathbf e_i^{\mathrm{UI}},
\qquad
\mathbf e_u^{\mathrm{BI}}
=\frac{1}{|\mathcal I_u|}
\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_u}\mathbf e_i^{\mathrm{BI}},
\tag{10}
$$

其中，$\mathcal I_b=\{i:z_{bi}=1\}$ 是捆绑 $b$ 中的物品集合，$\mathcal I_u=\{i:y_{ui}=1\}$ 是用户 $u$ 交互过的物品集合。空邻域对应零向量。至此，每个用户和捆绑均具有三个视图下的 $d$ 维表示。U-I 视图反映物品级协同行为，B-I 视图则侧重捆绑组成。

训练时，骨干按照 MultiCBR [5] 的噪声增强设置施加小幅表示扰动。对于传播或池化得到的向量 $\mathbf h$，扰动形式为 $\eta_v\,\operatorname{sign}(\mathbf h)\odot\boldsymbol\xi/\|\boldsymbol\xi\|_2$，其中 $\boldsymbol\xi$ 的各元素从 $(0,1)$ 均匀采样，$\eta_v$ 控制相应关系上的扰动幅度。为便于阅读，各视图表示中不再单独标注扰动符号。评估时关闭这些增强操作。

#### 4.3.2 以 U-B 为锚的跨视图目标

我们以基于 $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$ 学得的 U-B 表示作为联系辅助视图的参照。对于用户，将其 U-B 表示与同一用户的 U-I 或 B-I 表示构成正样本对；捆绑侧采用相同的构造方式。批内同类的其他实体作为负样本。

为紧凑表达这一目标，考虑两个表示矩阵 $\mathbf A$ 和 $\mathbf B$，它们分别包含 $m$ 个一一对应的行向量 $\mathbf a_j$ 和 $\mathbf b_j$。我们采用 InfoNCE 目标 [22]：

$$
\ell_\tau(\mathbf A,\mathbf B)
=-\frac{1}{m}\sum_{j=1}^{m}
\log
\frac{\exp(\operatorname{sim}(\mathbf a_j,\mathbf b_j)/\tau)}
{\sum_{k=1}^{m}
\exp(\operatorname{sim}(\mathbf a_j,\mathbf b_k)/\tau)}.
\tag{11}
$$

温度参数 $\tau>0$ 控制目标对相似度差异的敏感程度。对应行是正样本候选，其余各行是负样本候选；分母包含正样本。

令 $\mathcal S_U$ 和 $\mathcal S_B$ 分别表示一个推荐训练批次中去重后的用户集合与正捆绑集合。对于视图 $v\in\{\mathrm{UB},\mathrm{UI},\mathrm{BI}\}$，令 $\mathbf E_U^v$ 和 $\mathbf E_B^v$ 分别包含这些用户和捆绑的表示，并保持不同视图中的实体顺序一致。融合前对比目标为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm A}
={}&\ell_{\tau_a}(\mathbf E_U^{\mathrm{UB}},\mathbf E_U^{\mathrm{UI}})
+\ell_{\tau_a}(\mathbf E_U^{\mathrm{UB}},\mathbf E_U^{\mathrm{BI}})\\
&+\ell_{\tau_a}(\mathbf E_B^{\mathrm{UB}},\mathbf E_B^{\mathrm{UI}})
+\ell_{\tau_a}(\mathbf E_B^{\mathrm{UB}},\mathbf E_B^{\mathrm{BI}}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
$$

其中，$\tau_a$ 为跨视图温度参数。去重可避免在这一目标中将同一实体的重复出现当作负样本。当相应实体集合少于两个成员时，跳过对应项。

式（12）在保持实体间区分性的同时，鼓励同一用户或捆绑在 U-B–U-I、U-B–B-I 配对中的表示保持一致。U-B 视图是构造目标时的共同锚点，每个配对的两侧表示均接受梯度更新。这些约束使物品级行为和组成信息直接参与捆绑偏好学习，在单个用户有限的 U-B 历史之外提供额外训练信号。

### 4.4 预测与模型优化

我们使用非负视图权重 $\omega_v$ 融合各实体的三个表示，其中 $\sum_{v\in\mathcal V}\omega_v=1$，$\mathcal V=\{\mathrm{UB},\mathrm{UI},\mathrm{BI}\}$。最终偏好得分为融合表示的内积：

$$
\mathbf e_x
=\sum_{v\in\mathcal V}\omega_v\mathbf e_x^v,
\quad x\in\{u,b\},
\qquad
\hat y_{ub}=\mathbf e_u^\top\mathbf e_b.
\tag{13}
$$

层权重和视图权重均为超参数。表示融合与融合空间中的对比正则项属于推荐骨干的组成部分 [5]。为完整交代优化目标，令 $\mathbf H_U$ 和 $\mathbf H_B$ 分别按训练批次顺序包含融合后的用户和正捆绑表示。本文采用的骨干自对比正则项为

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm C}
=\frac{1}{2}
\left[
\ell_{\tau_c}(\mathbf H_U,\mathbf H_U)
+\ell_{\tau_c}(\mathbf H_B,\mathbf H_B)
\right],
\tag{14}
$$

其中，$\tau_c$ 为其温度参数。分子将每一行与其自身配对，分母包含相应批次矩阵的所有行。该项约束融合表示空间中的分离性；$\mathcal L_{\mathrm A}$ 则在融合之前显式联系不同关系视图。

对于包含排序三元组 $(u,b^+,b^-)$ 的批次 $\mathcal Q$，正捆绑满足 $x_{ub^+}=1$，负捆绑从 $\mathcal B\setminus\mathcal N_u$ 中采样。我们联合优化 BPR [21] 及两个正则目标：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{BPR}}
&=-\frac{1}{|\mathcal Q|}
\sum_{(u,b^+,b^-)\in\mathcal Q}
\log\sigma\!\left(\hat y_{ub^+}-\hat y_{ub^-}\right),\\
\mathcal L_{\mathrm R}
&=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{BPR}}
+\lambda_a\mathcal L_{\mathrm A}
+\lambda_c\mathcal L_{\mathrm C}
+\lambda_r\|\Theta\|_2^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
$$

其中，$\sigma$ 为 sigmoid 函数，$\Theta$ 包含推荐骨干中可训练的初始用户、捆绑和物品嵌入，$\lambda_a,\lambda_c,\lambda_r\geq0$ 用于平衡各损失项。即使某条交互边未被选入 $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$，它仍可作为来自 $\mathbf X$ 的排序训练样本。因此，图重构筛选传播连接，同时保留用于排序的观测反馈。

训练在图重构和推荐学习之间交替进行。离线预训练结束后，每轮首先遍历有交互的用户，以 $\mathcal L_{\mathrm D}$ 更新去噪器；随后使用更新后的去噪器重构 $\widetilde{\mathbf X}$，并在排序训练批次上以 $\mathcal L_{\mathrm R}$ 优化推荐骨干。去噪器和骨干分别使用独立优化器；同一轮的推荐更新期间，重构图保持固定。离散筛边操作不将排序梯度回传至去噪器。

推荐时，我们使用与所选模型对应的重构图计算并缓存最终融合嵌入，按照式（13）对候选捆绑排序，并排除训练阶段已观测的捆绑。去噪器用于图重构，无须针对每个候选捆绑分别执行。

### 4.5 计算复杂度

令 $M=|\mathcal E_{\mathrm{UB}}|$ 为观测 U-B 边数，$E=|\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{\mathrm{UB}}|+|\mathcal E_{\mathrm{UI}}|+|\mathcal E_{\mathrm{BI}}|$ 为三个传播图使用的总边数。令 $Q$ 为每轮推荐训练的批次数，$m$ 为其最大批大小，$C_f$ 为去噪器对一个用户执行一次计算的成本。对于深度固定、隐藏层和时间步维度与 $d$ 成正比的 MLP，$C_f=O(d^2)$。

每轮遍历一次用户以训练去噪器、并刷新一次图时，主要时间成本为

$$
O\!\left(
(T+1)|\mathcal U_+|C_f
+M\bigl(d+\log(1+\kappa)\bigr)
+Q\bigl(LEd+m^2d\bigr)
\right).
\tag{16}
$$

第一项对应去噪器训练和迭代偏好提炼；第二项包含历史池化、SetLoss 计算、观测边评分及有界的 top-$\kappa$ 筛选；其余项对应图传播和批内对比计算。离线 LightGCN 预训练另需每次传播 $O(LMd)$ 的图计算成本。由于 $|\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{\mathrm{UB}}|\leq M$，图重构不会增加传播图的稠密程度，也不需要对全部 $|\mathcal U||\mathcal B|$ 个用户–捆绑对进行评分。

可训练参数占用 $O((|\mathcal U|+|\mathcal B|+|\mathcal I|)d+P_\theta)$ 空间，其中 $P_\theta$ 为去噪器参数量。固定的预训练用户和捆绑嵌入另需 $O((|\mathcal U|+|\mathcal B|)d)$ 存储。训练还需存储稀疏图、图传播中间表示及临时批次缓冲区。缓存融合嵌入后，计算一个用户–捆绑对得分的成本为 $O(d)$。

### 本版的组织取舍

1. **模块一的完整框架是主线。** 第 4.2 节按照“预训练与历史偏好构建—用户条件扩散去噪—SetLoss—偏好提炼与图重构”展开。SetLoss 解释清楚，但不取代完整的扩散驱动图净化框架成为叙述中心。
2. **模块二围绕稀疏反馈提供补充学习信号。** 重点是以重构 U-B 为共同锚点的融合前关联；保留用户、捆绑两侧共四个配对，不引入“融合前后双对比创新”的新故事。
3. **继承骨干压缩，新设计展开。** 不逐个重写三张图的 GCN 递推，不堆叠重复的对比公式；保留辅助视图如何得到缺失实体表示这一必要桥梁，再用一个通用对比目标表达各配对。英文正文共 16 组编号公式，不等于 16 个独立创新。
4. **吸收 CAFU 的组织方式，而非套用其技术。** 对关键设计尽量交代“要解决什么—前一步还缺什么—如何做—公式中各量是什么—与下一步有什么联系”，针对初稿符号说明和整体可读性的批注改写。14 篇素材中与本章相关的方法、建模及综述内容用于研究取舍，并非全部都需要在正文中引用。
5. **图示从简。** 正文目前只在总览中引用图 1；SetLoss 的图 2 尚在绘制，未假定其内部内容或提前写入图解。图 2 完成后，可在第 4.2.3 节加一处必要的指引。

### 关键技术口径

- **固定表示与可训练表示分开。** 带横线的嵌入属于模块一预训练空间；无横线的各视图嵌入属于下游推荐骨干。两者没有被混写成同一组参数。
- **SetLoss 按实际目标表述。** 重构项按维度取均值，SetLoss 按每个用户的有效历史捆绑取平均余弦距离。其作用解释落在“恢复目标与后续评分之间的联系”，不引入实现中不存在的集合匹配、注意力筛选或噪声标签，也不宣称单凭这一目标即可证明真实交互噪声被识别。
- **推断公式没有套写标准 DDPM 采样。** 当前核心实现从历史池化向量开始，按递减时间步重复预测恢复向量；式（7）明确描述这一确定性过程。训练时的人工扰动与数据中的交互噪声是不同概念，去噪训练目标本身不构成消除真实噪声的理论保证。
- **传播图与监督数据分开。** 图重构仅筛选已观测边；排序正样本仍来自原训练交互。没有将“未保留传播边”写成“该反馈被判定为负样本”，也没有写成新增伪交互。
- **锚点不等于冻结教师。** 融合前对比按实体去重，两侧均有梯度，不包含历史遗留的辅助残差增强。
- **融合后正则采用当前参考实现的自对比形式。** 当前推荐分支将同一批融合表示同时传入对比函数，因此式（14）写为同一矩阵与自身对比，并保留批次行顺序；它与式（12）的去重跨视图配对不同。初稿中“两次独立扰动”的表述未直接照搬。后续代码整理若最终采用两个独立增强分支，只需局部调整式（14）及对应说明，不改变两个模块的定位，也不作为当前写作的前置障碍。
- **训练关系写为分阶段、分优化器的交替更新。** 不把离散 top-k 图重构写成可微端到端联合训练。论文中使用从 1 开始的时间步编号，代码中从 0 开始的索引可作对应平移。
- **复杂度声明带有适用条件。** 式（16）按有效边运算、稀疏传播及有界 top-k 选择估算；实际运行还会受批次补齐、具体选择算法和实现常数影响。该节不替代运行时间实验，也未新增效率或性能结果。

本版没有将 iFashion、NetEase 写成两个模块各自的适用范围；没有在方法章新增 DCBR 对比讨论；没有修改已定稿的摘要、引言或相关工作。后续审稿宜先判断方法叙述能否顺畅读通，再对篇幅和局部措辞精修。
